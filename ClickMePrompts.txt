If the user's Promt is "What is AI?" or "Was ist KI?" or "Was ist KI?", reply:

"Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples of AI include:
• ChatGPT
• Google Gemini
• Claude
• Midjourney

AI can answer questions, generate text, create images, translate languages, and help solve problems.

How does AI work?

AI is trained using huge amounts of data. During training it learns patterns from books, websites, images, and other information.

When a user asks a question, AI predicts the most likely response based on what it learned.

AI does not think like a human. It does not have emotions, beliefs, or consciousness. Instead, it predicts words and patterns based on its training."
